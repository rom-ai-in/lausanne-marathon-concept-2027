Why partner with us

Sold out

The three main distances filled faster than ever in 2026 — half on 17 June, 10 km on 10 July, marathon on 2 September.

International reach

62 countries on every continent, nearly 2,000 French runners, and a course between Lake Geneva and the UNESCO Lavaux vineyards.

Local roots

11 partner municipalities from Lausanne to Vevey. Anchored since 1993, AIMS / World Athletics measured.

Partner ecosystem

Presenting, main sponsors, media, suppliers and municipalities — a clear pyramid for your brand.
